---

Solvagio is an independent software studio. We build a small family of products — get
paid, lock down your domain, keep the bots out, and more — for the businesses and people
who use them every day. Built carefully, documented properly, and we treat the data you
trust us with as yours, not ours.

Every product is available **managed (SaaS) or self-hosted** — same software either way.

## Products

**Available now**

- **Sallyport** — a webhook tunnel that lives on your own server.
- **Postern** — see who's sending mail as your domain.
- **Moat** — spam protection and contact forms, with no third-party in the middle.
- **KeyForge** — license key management that doesn't fight you.

**In the works**

- **Coffer** — Cyprus-compliant invoicing and VAT records, under one roof. *(In development)*
- **Castellan** — one governed gate for every agent-to-tool interaction. *(Design preview)*
- **Mosaily** — every guest's photos of the day, in one private gallery. *(Coming soon)*

## Services

- **Web design & development** — hand-built, fast, accessible sites; no bloated CMS, no
  third-party trackers.
- **Hosting** — EU-based managed hosting with HTTPS, security headers, and optional email.

## How we build

- **One job, done well.** Each product keeps a small surface area you can actually
  understand — no sprawling all-in-one suite.
- **Documentation is the product.** Clear docs and honest status labels. What you see is
  what ships — no guesswork, no surprises.
- **Your data stays yours.** No third parties in the middle. We never treat the data you
  trust us with as a side business.
